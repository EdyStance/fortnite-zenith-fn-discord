Community Guidelines

Please respect these rules while you're part of the Zenith community.

01

🤝 Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and personal attacks are not allowed.

RESPECT
02

💬 Keep Chat Appropriate

Keep conversations suitable for the community. Avoid excessive offensive language, spam and disruptive behavior.

CHAT
03

🚫 No Spam

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, unnecessary mentions or excessive reactions.

NO SPAM
04

🛡️ No Cheating

Cheats, exploits and unfair advantages are not allowed in community events or games.

FAIR PLAY
05

📢 No Unapproved Advertising

Do not advertise servers, websites, social accounts or other communities without staff permission.

ADVERTISING
06

🔒 Respect Privacy

Never share another person's private information. Respect everyone's privacy and personal boundaries.

PRIVACY
07

🎮 Play Fair

Follow the rules of community games and events. Do not intentionally ruin the experience for other players.

GAMING
08

👮 Respect Staff

Follow reasonable staff instructions. If you disagree with a decision, contact staff calmly instead of causing drama.

STAFF
09

⚠️ No Malicious Behavior

Do not attempt to damage, disrupt or interfere with the community, website or other members.

SECURITY
10

🧠 Use Common Sense

Not every situation can be listed in the rules. Use good judgment and help keep Zenith a positive community.

COMMON SENSE
⚡

Rules Are Enforced

Breaking the rules may result in a warning, mute, kick or ban depending on the situation and its severity. Staff decisions should be respected.
