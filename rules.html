RULES

Please read and respect the rules of the Zenith community.

01

🤝 Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, personal attacks and unnecessary conflicts are not allowed.

02

💬 No Swearing

Keep the server friendly and avoid offensive or inappropriate language.

03

🛡️ Anti-Cheat

Cheats, exploits and unfair advantages are not allowed. Always play fair.

04

📢 No Spam

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, unnecessary mentions or spam.

05

🚫 No Advertising

Advertising other servers, communities or services without permission is not allowed.

06

🔞 No NSFW

Inappropriate or NSFW content is not allowed anywhere in the Zenith community.

07

😐 No Toxic Behavior

Do not intentionally start arguments, bully members or create unnecessary drama.

08

💰 No Scamming

Scamming, fraud attempts and attempts to deceive other members are strictly prohibited.

09

👮 Respect Staff

Follow reasonable instructions from the Zenith staff team and communicate respectfully.

10

🎭 No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another member, staff member or another person.

11

📌 Use Channels Correctly

Keep conversations in the appropriate channels and help us keep the server organized.

12

⚠️ Breaking Rules

Breaking the rules may result in a warning, mute, kick or ban depending on the situation.

By staying in the server, you confirm that you have read and agreed to follow the Zenith rules.
