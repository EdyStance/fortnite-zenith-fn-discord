📜 ZENITH COMMUNITY RULES

SERVER RULES

Please read all of the rules before participating in the Zenith community. By joining the server, you agree to follow these rules. Staff members may take action when necessary to keep the community safe and enjoyable.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, personal attacks and intentionally causing problems are not allowed. Everyone should be able to enjoy the community.

02

No Swearing

Keep the chat clean and appropriate. Excessive swearing, insults and offensive language are not allowed. Example: repeatedly using offensive words toward another member. If you swear or insult other members, you may receive a warning, mute or ban depending on the situation.

03

Anti-Cheat

Cheats, hacks, exploits, unauthorized software, unfair advantages and attempts to bypass game security are strictly prohibited. Using cheats can result in an immediate ban from the community.

04

No Spam

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, excessive emojis, repeated mentions or unnecessary content. Keep conversations readable and useful for everyone.

05

No Advertising

Advertising other Discord servers, websites, communities, products or services without permission is not allowed. Ask staff before posting promotional content.

06

No NSFW Content

Keep the community safe and appropriate. Sexual, explicit or otherwise inappropriate content is not allowed anywhere in the server.

07

No Toxic Behavior

Do not intentionally provoke arguments, start unnecessary drama or repeatedly target other members. Competitive gaming is fine, but keep it friendly.

08

No Scamming

Scamming, phishing, stealing accounts, impersonating staff or attempting to trick members is strictly prohibited. Any suspicious activity should be reported to the staff team.

09

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff members are responsible for maintaining order. If a moderator asks you to stop an activity or move a conversation, follow the instruction. If you believe a decision was unfair, contact staff privately.

10

No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another member, moderator, administrator, creator or official representative. Impersonation can result in an immediate moderation action.

11

Keep Channels On Topic

Use channels for their intended purpose. Read channel descriptions and keep conversations relevant so that everyone can easily find the information they need.
