SERVER RULES

Please read all rules before participating in the Zenith community. By joining the server, you agree to follow these rules. Staff may take action when necessary.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, personal attacks and intentionally causing problems are not allowed.

02

No Swearing

Keep the chat clean and appropriate. Offensive language, insults and excessive swearing are not allowed. If you insult or swear at members, you may receive a warning, mute or ban depending on the situation.

03

Anti-Cheat

Cheats, hacks, unauthorized software, exploits and other unfair advantages are strictly prohibited. Using cheats can result in an immediate ban.

04

No Spam

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, excessive emojis, repeated mentions or unnecessary content.

05

No Advertising

Advertising other Discord servers, websites, communities or services without permission is not allowed.

06

No NSFW Content

Keep the community safe and appropriate. Explicit or inappropriate content is not allowed anywhere in the server.

07

No Toxic Behavior

Do not intentionally provoke arguments, create drama or repeatedly target other members. Keep competition friendly.

08

No Scamming

Scamming, phishing, stealing accounts, impersonating staff or attempting to trick members is strictly prohibited.

09

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff members help maintain order. Follow reasonable moderator instructions and contact staff privately if you believe a decision was unfair.

10

No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another member, moderator, administrator or official representative.

11

Keep Channels On Topic

Use channels for their intended purpose. Keep conversations relevant so information remains easy to find.

12

Breaking Rules

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, staff may issue a warning, mute, kick or ban. Serious violations may result in an immediate ban.
